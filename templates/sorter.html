How it works

Choose groups, then expand them to include only the idols you want. Switch to Groups to rank whole groups instead.

Pick your favorite in each pair, or tie them. Everyone gets several matchups before we spend extra choices refining your favorites. You get a complete ranking, with more precision near the top. Opening matchups loosely mix leaderboard positions with randomness; your ranking comes from your own answers. Finished? Refine your favorites with extra matchups, including challengers outside the top 10.

Every idol pick (except ties) counts as one vote on the global leaderboard. Your finished ranking shows up under Mine — exactly as the sorter called it. Undo fixes your ranking, but votes already counted stay counted. A pairing moves global ELO once a day no matter how often you re-pick it, and weight fades past about 250 pairings a day so no single session moves the shared boards by itself. Group-mode duels are just for fun and don't vote.

Your session saves on this device after every choice. Undo works all the way back. Completed rankings stay on this device until you start a new ranking. Use “Copy progress link” to resume your current progress on another device. Links capture the choices made when copied; later choices do not sync between devices. Shared result links include your ranking.

Idol portraits come from our kpopping collection, with the classic sorter photos as backup.
